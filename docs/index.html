Measure rooms, send quotes

Quote the job before you leave the room. RoomQuote is built for painters, floor fitters and tilers: measure a room, pick the job, and send a professional PDF quote in a few minutes.

Measure

  • On iPhones with LiDAR, scan the room: walls, doors and windows are measured for you.
  • On any other iPhone, type length, width and height, plus doors and windows.

Price

  • Paint by coats, coverage and waste, rounded up to whole cans.
  • Flooring and tiles with a waste allowance, rounded up to whole boxes.
  • Labour per square metre or per hour, VAT on or off, metric or imperial.

Send

  • A clean PDF with your details, line items, total, validity and terms.
  • Send it on WhatsApp, by email, or save it to Files, and track it from Draft to Paid.

Private by design
