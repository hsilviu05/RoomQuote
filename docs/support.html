My iPhone can't scan rooms

Room scanning needs a LiDAR scanner (iPhone Pro and Pro Max models from iPhone 12 Pro onward, and recent iPad Pro). On every other iPhone, tap New quote, then Enter measurements and type the room's length, width and height.

The paint or box count looks high

RoomQuote adds your waste allowance, then rounds up to whole cans or boxes. Check Coats, Waste allowance and Coverage on the Room & materials screen. Doors and windows you add are taken off the wall area.

How do I add my logo?

Go to Settings › Logo. The logo is printed on PDF quotes with RoomQuote Pro.

How do I send a quote on WhatsApp?

Open the quote and tap Send on WhatsApp, then choose WhatsApp in the share sheet. You can also email it or save the PDF to Files.

Subscriptions

The free plan includes 3 quotes a month. To restore Pro on a new iPhone, tap Settings › Restore purchases. To cancel, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Apple handles all payments and refunds; request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. RoomQuote has no account or server, so we can't see or recover your data. Deleting the app deletes it. See the Privacy Policy.
